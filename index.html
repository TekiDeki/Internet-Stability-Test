Internet Stability Test

Runs continuous latency probes against a public endpoint, then tells you the truth about your connection: jitter, lost probes, outages - how stable it really is. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

no account nothing uploaded one HTML file

How the measurement works

  • Every few seconds a tiny request is sent to the chosen public endpoint and the round-trip time is recorded - like a ping, but over HTTPS, so values run slightly higher.
  • A probe that gets no answer within 4 seconds counts as lost. Three or more lost in a row count as an outage, and its duration is measured. Each probe waits for the previous one.
  • The loop runs in a web worker and the results stay in your browser. Nothing is uploaded and nothing leaves the page except the probes themselves.

Set up the test

The probe loop keeps running in a background tab; just keep the tab open. The screen-awake option needs a visible tab.

How the verdict is decided:
Stable: 0% lost, 0 outages, jitter under 15 ms, average under 150 ms.
Mostly stable: under 1% lost, 0 outages, jitter under 40 ms.
Unstable: under 5% lost and at most 2 outages.
Poor: anything worse, or downtime over 3% of the run.
